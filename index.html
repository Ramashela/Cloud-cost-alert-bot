Three parts, running quietly in the background

No dashboards to babysit — the bot comes to you.

01 · watch

Reads your cost APIs directly

Connects to AWS Cost Explorer or Azure Cost Management with read-only credentials. No agents, no exporters.

02 · compare

Checks against your budget

Set a monthly budget and an alert threshold per account. The bot tracks month-to-date spend against it automatically.

03 · notify

Sends the alert where you already work

Slack and Discord webhooks, delivered the moment a threshold is crossed — with a built-in cooldown so you're not spammed.
